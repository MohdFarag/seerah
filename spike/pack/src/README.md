# Spike test data. NOT historical content

Everything here is **synthetic test data**, made only to exercise the technology:

- Names are fictional (قرية الاختبار، منطقة حكم تجريبية …) and chosen to stress Arabic shaping: joined letters, full tashkeel, ﷺ, Arabic-Indic and Western digits, and mixed Latin text.
- "Years" are abstract **test years 1–6**, not Hijri or Gregorian dates.
- Shapes are deliberately geometric (rectangles, hexagons) so they can't be mistaken for real borders.
- Texts are neutral test sentences, not Quran, hadith or narrations. Sources are fake and marked `مصدر اختبار`.

Every feature and event carries `"test": true`.
