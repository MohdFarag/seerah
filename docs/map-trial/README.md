# Map trial

Trackers and step reports for the map trial required by [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-map-data-pipeline.md) decision 13 before building the first v1 map package.

- **Specification:** [docs/design/map-trial-build.md](../design/map-trial-build.md) (Approved 2026-11-14; option (c) ramp and fade amended 2026-11-17)
- **Implementation plans:**
  - [Phase 1: tool core and example](../design/map-trial-phase-1-plan.md) — merged 2026-11-16 (`tools/map/`, `map-data/example/`, `map` workflow and public toolchain image)
  - [Phase 2: the map on the device](../design/map-trial-phase-2-plan.md) — merged 2026-11-17 (`:core:map`, `benchmark` build type, `app.seerah.trial`, release guard)
  - [Phase 3: base layer, trial inputs and documents](../design/map-trial-phase-3-plan.md) — merged 2026-11-20 (`base.pmtiles`, `tippecanoe` and `WhiteboxTools` in the published `ghcr.io/mohdfarag/seerah-map:5498b0a66ee99ccb` image, `:core:map` base-layer wiring, documentation/notices)

> [!NOTE]
> **Temporary device deferral (as of 2026-11-22):** As recorded in [step-1.md](step-1.md) and [step-2.md](step-2.md), the Oppo A55 and Huawei MatePad 11.5 are temporarily unavailable for testing. Step 1 checks 1, 4 and 11 on both devices, as well as the Oppo A55 and MatePad measurements required for trial items 3 and 11, are deferred until those devices are available. Galaxy A54 Step 1 mechanics numbers are recorded in [step-1.md](step-1.md), and Galaxy A54 Step 2 window runs plus owner Step 2 decisions are recorded in [step-2.md](step-2.md).

## Step reports

- **[step-1.md](step-1.md) (Step 1 — Foundations):** records checks 1–12, the 2026-11-18 Galaxy A54 phase-2 run, and the 2026-11-21 GLO-90 valley probe. Owner approval and Step 2 builds/smoke check completed afterwards ([step-2.md](step-2.md)); deferred Oppo A55 and Huawei MatePad 11.5 checks 1, 4 and 11 remain until those devices are available.
- **[step-2.md](step-2.md) (Step 2 — Small windows):** records the 8 workstation window builds (`cb58036` / `15a4d7c`), Galaxy A54 cold-launch measurements across `window-najd`, `window-spike` (`zoom12` vs `per-zoom`) and `window-yemen`, Phase 3 `base.pmtiles` smoke check, Android 16 FUSE `adb push` note, and owner decisions choosing `option-c` as primary (`painted` `11.5–13.5` fade fallback). Oppo A55 and MatePad 11.5 device runs remain deferred.
- **[step-3.md](step-3.md) (Step 3 — 10% band extrapolation):** built `trial/band-10pct` (`144 / 1,435` blocks, `10.0%`) for `value` and `painted` (`0` OOMs); projections (`27.2–38.1 min`, `2.57 GB` temp disk, `1.28 GiB` `z12` / `0.49 GiB` `z11` for `value`; `43.4–54.3 min`, `0.94 GB` temp disk, `0.40 GiB` `z12` for `painted`) pass `12 h`, `120 GB` and `2 GiB`.
- `step-4.md` (Step 4 — Full data extent, not started): full-extent build and review on all three devices (items 1, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11).
- `step-5.md` (Step 5 — Valleys, not triggered): the step 1 valley probe exceeded the 8,192 MiB cap at both `180 m` and `90 m`, so the conditional valley layer trial does not run unless a different flow-routing approach is later proposed.
